# Arknights roguelike recruitment

Treat recruitment as a draft with a budget. First observe Hope, voucher class,
current squad roles, likely next difficulty spike, and every visible offer.
Write a short ledger before clicking: `Hope now → after`, `impact S/A/B/C`,
`free/cheap/expensive`, `carry/enabler/patch/skip`, and the next-node plan.

Keep Hope for future high-value vouchers. Do not buy the first acceptable card
just because it is visible. A high-rarity operator can be a poor purchase when
the squad cannot deploy or support it; a cheap operator can be correct when it
repairs a lethal gap. Prefer a carry, then an enabler or hard-check answer,
then a cheap repair. Use temporary filler only when the next fight demands it.

Unknown operators and relics need evidence from `web_search` or an owner
question. Never invent a tier or treat one lucky run as a rule. After each
choice record the offer, Hope delta, prediction, and outcome so Brain can raise
or lower confidence over repeated sessions.
